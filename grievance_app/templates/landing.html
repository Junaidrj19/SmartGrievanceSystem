{# -------------------------------------------------------- How it works #}

THE WORKFLOW

How It Works

Seven deliberate steps keep the right people informed and the case history intact.

  1. 01

    Submit

    A student files a grievance with its details.

  2. 02

    Reference

    The system generates a unique reference ID.

  3. 03

    Assign

    An administrator assigns a department and staff.

  4. 04

    Review

    Authorized staff review the assigned case.

  5. 05

    Update

    Status changes and remarks are recorded.

  6. 06

    Escalate

    Overdue unresolved cases escalate by policy.

  7. 07

    Resolve

    Resolution and closure are recorded when permitted.
