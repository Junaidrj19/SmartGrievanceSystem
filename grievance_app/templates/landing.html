THE WORKFLOW

A clear path from
voice to resolution.

Seven deliberate steps keep the right people informed and the case history intact.

  1. 01

    Sign in securely

    Use the appropriate seeded account to enter the workspace for your role.

    {{ icon('shield', 18) }}
  2. 02

    Submit the details

    Describe the issue with a title, category, description, and optional location.

    {{ icon('clipboard', 18) }}
  3. 03

    Receive your reference

    Resolve creates a unique grievance reference ID and response deadline.

    {{ icon('layers', 18) }}

    Assign responsibility

    Review and progress

    {{ icon('arrow', 18) }}
  6. 06

    Escalate when needed

    {{ icon('alert', 18) }}

    Resolve and close

    Resolution and final closure are recorded as part of the complete case history.
